## Important difference from v1
v1 rocks/grassDebris are photos on WHITE backgrounds, cut out at load time with Otsu thresholding. The new rock, grass/leaf and dirt snippets are already cut out: use the PNG alpha directly and DO NOT Otsu-threshold them (it would destroy the soft edges). Load with `Image.open(p).convert("RGBA")` (or `cv2.imread(p, cv2.IMREAD_UNCHANGED)`; BGRA) and composite with `alpha = a/255`.

## How each set was made
- backgrounds: each 1K texture is turned into a 2304x1296 mosaic of random 90-degree-rotated/flipped copies, cosine-feather-blended (overlapping tiles, random offsets, brightness-matched, contrast restored) to hide seams. Sources: ambientCG Ground, Gravel, Grass, Moss. 2304 px covers the generator's 1.0-1.5 zoom + 1920x1080 crop. Faint repetition can still be visible at 1.0 zoom. Real upsampling is none (1K tile used at 1:1).
- rocks: random crops of Rock/Gravel textures masked with a smooth-noise irregular blob (random elongation), 1-2 px soft edge, rim darkening (~38% at the very edge), cropped to bbox, padded to square canvas. Some rocks are small crops of fine gravel (look like granular lumps).
- grassDebris: ambientCG ScatteredLeaves maps are leaf layers on black; alpha = luminance>40 mask (opened, eroded 1px, blurred) times an organic blob envelope. Result is dry/autumn leaf-litter clippings (leaf clusters), not blades. The Grass* textures were judged too flat/synthetic-looking for snippets, so no blade clippings. Black fringes are mostly removed by the erosion; check composites on light backgrounds.
- dirt: crops of soil/sand/gravel Ground textures: ~1/3 "clod" (hard-ish edge, rim shading), ~1/3 "patch" (feathered), ~1/3 "dust" (very soft, max alpha ~0.7). The kind is in the filename (`dirt_clod_*`, `dirt_patch_*`, `dirt_dust_*`).
- Filenames end in the source AssetId.

## Caveats
Textures are close-up photogrammetry/scan colour maps (flat, albedo-like, lighting mostly baked out), not true drone aerial photos: scale, perspective and shadowing differ from the v1 backgrounds (which have strong sun/shadow dappling). Backgrounds are lower in contrast and a few are bright pink-beige sand. Rock snippets have no cast shadow.

## grassBlades/ (procedural, CC0-equivalent: generated by our own script)
No usable real blade photos were found (ambientCG Grass* maps are flat, Poly Haven plants are 3D scans), so blades are drawn procedurally by `tools/make_grass_blades.py`: `make_blade(seed, length=, width=, family=, bend=, droop=, twist=, orientation=)`, `make_tuft(seed, n_blades=, size=)`, `make_wisp(seed, size=)`; each returns a tight-cropped RGBA uint8 numpy array (H,W,4), seeded and deterministic, so the generator can create fresh ones on the fly. Antialiased (4x supersample, premultiplied downsample, no dark fringes), curved/drooping/twisting tapered polygon, darker edges, lighter midrib, tip translucency, vein noise, colour families green/olive/straw/tan with per-blade jitter. `python3 tools/make_grass_blades.py` regenerates the pool in grassBlades/ (blade_NN, tuft_NN, wisp_NN). `tools/test_strip.py bgA bgB bgC out.jpg` pastes blades at 1x onto 3 backgrounds for a quick plausibility check.

## Background pool skewed to grass (backgrounds_index.json)
`backgrounds_index.json` lists every background (v1 and new): `path`, `source` ("v1" = original assets/backgrounds dir, read-only; "new" = relative to assets_new/), `category` (grass_fresh, grass_olive, grass_dry, grass_patchy, moss_clover, soil, gravel, sand, mixed_ground), `family` (grass | non_grass), `weight`, `note`. Top-level `family_weights` = {grass 0.65, non_grass 0.35}. Items with weight 0 are dropped (flat grey, peach/cream sand). To sample: `fam = random.choices(["grass","non_grass"], [0.65,0.35])[0]`, then `random.choices(items_of_fam, weights=[w for ...])`, skipping weight 0. Sand/pink/flat-grey items are down-weighted (0.25-0.5). Usable counts by category (weight>0): grass_patchy 20, grass_olive 17, gravel 16, grass_fresh 15, grass_dry 14, soil 12, moss_clover 10, mixed_ground 6, sand 4 -> grass family 76 of 114 usable (66.7% by count).
Grass-family generation: `tools/grass_backgrounds.py` (20 real ambientCG Grass/Moss textures mosaicked, desaturated and given low-frequency brightness patchiness, dry/soil patches and optional sun/shade dappling; 40 procedural lawns: thousands of blades from make_blade scattered over a soil texture with density/dryness maps, 3 depth passes with ambient-occlusion darkening, optional clover), `tools/build_index.py` (hand classification), `tools/sheet2.py`-style contact sheet. Procedural lawns are stylised: blades are thin and sharp compared with real turf and the dry/patchy ones lean strongly straw-coloured; the dappling is smooth-noise and rather soft.

## Dirt update (soft edges, index)
- All dirt snippets were re-processed with `tools/soften_alpha.py`: `soften_alpha(rgba, width_px, noise_scale, seed, dissolve=1.0)` (importable, seeded): distance-transform feather of 3-12 px (scaled to snippet size), noise dissolve in the rim only (grains/crumbs), colour bleed of the rim (no dark/white fringes); interior untouched. The generator can call it at composite time with a random width per use.
- `dirt/index.json`: per file `kind`, `opacity` range, `casts_shadow`, source, size. clod = raised lump, draw a small soft shadow, opacity 0.9-1.0; patch = flat soil/dust patch, alpha overlay only, no shadow, 0.6-0.95; dust = very soft, 0.15-0.5.
- Moved to rocks/ (they read as stones): rock_mossy_Ground001, rock_grey_Ground032, rock_beige_Ground005, rock_dark_Ground018 (soft rock edges, rim darkening).
- Dropped (kept only in dirt/_orig_sharp/): clod_18_Ground027 (cream blob), patch_16_Ground021 (blue-grey gravel), patch_19_Ground030 (pale grey), dust_20_Ground033 (cream blob), patch_07_Ground026 (peach), dust_05_Ground020 (gravel/debris flecks).
- 18 new soil snippets (7 clod, 6 patch, 5 dust; names dirt_<kind>_nNN_<Ground>.png) from ambientCG soil maps. Caveat: some small clods are a bit blurry (1K source upscaled/blended) and a few read as dry dirt crust; dark Ground002 has pale specks.
- Contact sheet shows dirt on checkerboard and on green, plus 2x zoom sections for wisps and blades.
